这是我的博士论文方向：船厂分段的占路运输。一句话：船厂里载着分段的平板车不是在路上跑，而是把路占了，所以运力的天花板在路网，不在车队。这个方向在 2026 年 10 月 8 日确定，目前已在玉浦和烟台两座船厂上做完立旗实验。今天汇报研究问题、理论路线、初步结果和接下来的计划。

This is my thesis direction: road-occupying block transport in shipyards. In one sentence, a loaded transporter in a shipyard does not drive on the road, it owns the road, so capacity is capped by the network rather than the fleet. The direction was set on 8 October 2026, and the flag experiment is done on two yards, Okpo and Yantai. Today: the question, the theory plan, first results and the next steps.

제 박사 논문 방향은 조선소 블록의 점유 운송입니다. 한 문장으로, 조선소에서 블록을 실은 트랜스포터는 도로를 달리는 것이 아니라 도로를 점유하므로, 운송 능력의 상한은 차량군이 아니라 도로망에 있습니다. 방향은 2026년 10월 8일에 확정했고 옥포와 옌타이 두 조선소에서 깃발 실험을 마쳤습니다. 오늘은 연구 질문, 이론 계획, 초기 결과, 다음 계획을 보고합니다.

被质疑的前提The premise의문을 제기하는 전제

船厂分段运输调度几乎都建立在“自由流路网”上：行驶时间取自距离矩阵，车辆之间互不影响。Shipyard transport scheduling almost always assumes a free-flow network: travel times from a distance matrix, vehicles that never interact.조선소 블록 운송 스케줄링은 거의 모두 자유류 도로망을 가정한다: 거리 행렬로 주행 시간을 정하고 차량끼리 영향이 없다.

已有结果First results초기 결과

两座船厂的吞吐都进入由路网决定、与车数无关的平台。玉浦 15→30 台时，每多一台车的吞吐只剩自由流的 58%→34%；搭载高峰的坞口是局部瓶颈。Both yards reach a throughput plateau set by the network, not the fleet. On Okpo, from 15 to 30 vehicles each extra unit adds only 58%→34% of its free-flow gain; the dock mouth is a local bottleneck at erection peaks.두 조선소 모두 처리량이 차량 수와 무관하게 도로망이 정하는 평탄 구간에 이른다. 옥포는 15→30대에서 한 대 추가 효과가 자유류의 58%→34%뿐이고, 탑재 피크의 도크 입구가 국지적 병목이다.

先看一个物理事实。空车平台宽六到七米半，但载着的分段常在十五米以上，比道路还宽。所以载货车一上路，对向和交叉的车都得等；转弯时还会扫过整个路口。最大的运输车空车也占路。这种载货和空驶、大车和小车之间的占路差，是船厂特有的，AGV 和仓储机器人里很弱。这几项尺寸来自报道，引用前还要核对原文。

First a physical fact. Empty platforms are six to seven and a half metres wide, but the blocks they carry are often more than fifteen metres, wider than the road. Once a transporter is loaded, oncoming and crossing vehicles must wait, and a turn sweeps the whole junction. The largest carriers occupy the road even when empty. This gap between loaded and empty, and between large and small carriers, is particular to shipyards. The dimensions come from press reports and will be checked against the originals.

核心问题只有一句：载货车辆独占道路时，运力由什么决定，车队、调度和路网该怎样协同设计。它改变的是对运输能力本身的理解。原来认为运输能力就是车队能力，调度就是派车，车越多越好；现在载货车辆是会移动的道路独占者，运力是车队和路网中较小的那个。在路网约束区，买车不如改路，派车不如排路，加车甚至会让系统变慢。

The core question in one sentence: when loaded vehicles own the road, what sets capacity, and how should fleet, scheduling and network be designed together? It changes how we understand transport capacity itself. The usual view equates capacity with the fleet, scheduling with dispatching, and more vehicles with better service. In the occupancy view, loaded vehicles are moving road owners and capacity is the smaller of fleet and network. Where the network binds, fixing a road beats buying a vehicle, scheduling the road beats dispatching, and more vehicles can slow the system.

Seven hypotheses were fixed before the experiments, and each yields a publishable conclusion whether it holds or not. For H1, if yards are network-bound, network-bound capacity exists; if not, we give a density criterion for when free flow suffices, which is also a boundary result. This is not defence; it makes bold claims credible. H1 has been restated piecewise from the two yards' readings, as shown later.

模型骨架借了铁路闭塞的思路。道路图上的路段和路口都是可独占资源；一次载货运输沿路径依次占用每个资源一段阻塞时间，也就是通过时间加净空时间，路口还包括转弯扫过的时间。起讫点的升降和对位要占用节点十到三十分钟。空驶按车型分档。路宽不去量，可通行类别作为情景参数，结论写成可交会路段比例的函数，这样只用公开数据也能做。

The model borrows from railway signalling. Segments and junctions of the road graph are exclusive resources; a loaded move holds each resource on its path for a blocking time, passage plus clearance, and at junctions the turn sweep is included. Lifting and positioning hold the end nodes for ten to thirty minutes. Empty runs are treated by vehicle class. Road widths are not measured: passability classes are scenario parameters and results are functions of the passable share, so public data suffice.

The theory uses only graph theory, linear programming, max-plus algebra, sample paths and constructions. Yards run a dozen to thirty vehicles, so exact finite-size results are more useful than asymptotics. T1, two bounds; T3, unbounded free-flow error; and T6, the exchange rate, are the easiest and come first; together they are the theory of the first paper. T2, the trapezoid; T4, non-monotonicity; and T5, safe orchestration, add to it.

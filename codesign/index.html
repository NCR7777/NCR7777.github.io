算法是双层的。外层选改造组合和车队，包括台数和车型；内层用第 4 章的编排去评估，得到所需车数和平台与上界之比。中间用对偶价格给外层的候选排序，只把前几名送去仿真，这样搜索量会小很多；上界与价格表最大的情景也只要约 6 分钟。内层现在用整条路径预约，更不保守的规则出来以后替换。底下是分界规则：如果平台远低于上界，就先回到第 4 章改编排；接近上界时按对偶价格改路，所需车数之差就是汇率。成果阶梯上，地板是网格评估，目标是汇率与关键路段识别，天花板是带最优性间隙的双层算法。

The algorithm is bilevel. The outer level chooses a set of road changes and a fleet, size and vehicle type; the inner level evaluates it with chapter 4's orchestration and returns vehicles needed and the plateau-to-bound ratio. Dual prices rank the outer candidates in between, and only the top few go to simulation, which shrinks the search; the bounds and price table take about 6 minutes even for the largest scenario. The inner level uses whole-route reservation for now and switches to a less conservative rule once it is ready. Underneath sits the dividing rule: if the plateau is far below the bound, go back to chapter 4 and improve orchestration; near the bound, change roads in dual-price order, and the difference in vehicles needed is the exchange rate. On the results ladder the floor is a grid evaluation, the target the exchange rate with critical links, and the ceiling a bilevel algorithm with optimality gaps.

The vehicle-type hypothesis is about how vehicle type interacts with occupancy. A large vehicle holds the lane whether loaded or empty; a small one can yield when empty where passing is possible, but a giant block needs several small vehicles, each running empty to meet, so empty running grows. The fleet-sizing paper found, in a model without traffic, that for every block one large enough vehicle is cheaper than any coupled team. The hypothesis asks whether occupancy changes that. The two nearest same-field papers, Tao et al. 2017 comparing vehicle types by simulation and Li et al. 2020 on two-vehicle cooperative transport, both leave the team's road holding out of the model; that is what the hypothesis adds. The case is the floating dock: Hanwha Ocean publishes 3 floating docks, and Okpo lifts giant blocks into them with sea cranes; a floating dock is a quay-side sink plus a floating-crane resource, with giant blocks carried by 2 to 4 coupled vehicles, in the floating-dock scenario after the exchange-rate table. “About 10 giant blocks per ship” comes from another yard, which the thesis will say when it uses the figure.

路—车汇率Road–vehicle exchange rate도로–차량 환율
一项道路改造相当于多少台车的运力：上界的对偶价格除以每台车每天的运力（路—车汇率定理）。铁路上的对应做法：Jensen 等 2020 用分支定界求路网容量，评价两处平交改立交，容量 +12.5%–37.5%。How many vehicles' worth of capacity a road change adds: the bound's dual price divided by one vehicle's daily capacity (the exchange-rate theorem).

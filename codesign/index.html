The algorithm is bilevel. The outer level chooses a set of road changes and a fleet, size and vehicle type; the inner level evaluates it with chapter 4's orchestration and returns vehicles needed and the plateau-to-bound ratio. Dual prices rank the outer candidates in between, and only the top few go to simulation, which shrinks the search. Underneath sits the dividing rule: if the plateau is far below the bound, go back to chapter 4 and improve orchestration; near the bound, change roads in dual-price order, and the difference in vehicles needed is the exchange rate.

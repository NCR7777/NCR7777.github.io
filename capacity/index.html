为什么下沿取整条路径预约，而不是新做的安全放行。安全放行在每次放行前检查：放行以后，是否还存在一个次序让路上的车依次走完剩下的任务；这是操作系统里的银行家算法，对应现场信号员的放行。它是精确的，86 万次判定和朴素算法逐次相同，所有批次没有一次死锁。但它保守：它按每辆车整段剩余任务来认领，不看时间，大约三分之二的等待是被判据拒绝的，审查给出的六入口环形算例里，三辆车同向绕环、根本不会相遇，也被拒了。所以 K 在 15 以下两者相当，20 以上安全放行就低于预约，曲线还先升后降。下沿因此取预约；T27 正在检验更不保守、仍然可靠的规则，下沿可能往上走。

Why the floor uses whole-route reservation rather than the new safe-release rule. Before each grant, safe release checks that afterwards there is still an order in which the vehicles on the road can finish their remaining tasks one by one; this is the banker's algorithm from operating systems, and it mirrors signalmen granting passage in the field. It is exact: 860,000 decisions match a naive reduction one by one, and no batch ever deadlocked. But it is conservative: it claims each vehicle's whole remaining task and ignores timing, so about two thirds of its waits are rejections by the criterion, and in the six-entrance ring case from the review three vehicles following each other round the ring, never meeting, are still refused.

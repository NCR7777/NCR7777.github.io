这是博士论文第 3 章的分项汇报：运力区间与路网约束区。我在两座真实布局的船厂上做了立旗实验，回答本研究的两项论断和两项假设：双上界、梯形律、路网约束区假设和自由流误差假设。一句话：运力不是一个数，而是一个区间，下沿由最好的无死锁交通规则决定，上沿由路网决定。这一版换上了 10 月 9 日至 10 日通过复核的三轮结果：物理规则与全厂任务量、系统上界与路线自由上界、外部锚点与文献核查，数字都来自本研究的仿真。

This is the sub-study briefing for chapter 3 of the thesis: the capacity interval and the network-bound regime. I ran the flag experiment on two real yard layouts to answer two claims and two hypotheses of this study: the two ceilings, the trapezoid law, the network-bound hypothesis and the free-flow error hypothesis. In one sentence: capacity is not one number but an interval, whose floor is set by the best deadlock-free traffic rule and whose ceiling is set by the network. This version brings in three rounds of results that passed review on 9 and 10 October: the physical rules and whole-yard volumes, the system and route-free bounds, and the external anchors with the literature check; all numbers come from the simulations of this study.

Why the floor uses whole-route reservation rather than the new safe-release rule. Before each grant, safe release checks that afterwards there is still an order in which the vehicles on the road can finish their remaining tasks one by one; this is the banker's algorithm from operating systems, and it mirrors signalmen granting passage in the field. It is exact: 860,000 decisions match a naive reduction one by one, and no batch ever deadlocked. But it is conservative: it claims each vehicle's whole remaining task and ignores timing, so about two thirds of its waits are rejections by the criterion, and in the six-entrance ring case three vehicles following each other round the ring, never meeting, are still refused. So up to K = 15 the two rules are on a par, from K = 20 safe release falls below reservation, and its curve rises and then falls. The floor therefore uses reservation; I am now testing a less conservative rule that is still reliable, which may lift the floor.

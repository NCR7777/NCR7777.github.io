这份分项汇报讲博士论文第 3 章的坞口一节：搭载高峰时，坞口，也就是吊车和坞前停靠路段串联起来的地方，什么时候会成为全厂的瓶颈。一句话：吊车排队本身不一定拖累全厂，关键是等吊车的车停在哪里。右图是示意：载着分段的车在坞前道路上等吊车，整条路都被占住，相邻的总组场地就进不去；如果车在路外等，路就是空的。

This sub-study covers the dock-mouth section of chapter 3: at erection peaks, when does the dock mouth, the crane and the dock stopping road in series, become the bottleneck of the whole yard? In one sentence: a crane queue need not hurt the yard; what matters is where the vehicles waiting for the crane stand. The sketch: a loaded transporter waits for the crane on the dock road, the whole road is held, and the neighbouring assembly site is cut off; if it waits off-road, the road stays free.

이 세부 보고는 박사 논문 3장의 도크 입구 절입니다. 탑재 피크에 도크 입구, 즉 크레인과 도크 앞 정차 구간이 직렬로 이어진 곳이 언제 조선소 전체의 병목이 되는지를 다룹니다. 한 문장으로, 크레인 대기열 자체가 전체를 해치는 것은 아니며 크레인을 기다리는 차가 어디에 서 있느냐가 관건입니다. 오른쪽은 개념도입니다: 블록을 실은 차가 도크 앞 도로에서 크레인을 기다리면 도로 전체가 점유되어 인접 총조립장에 들어갈 수 없고, 도로 밖에서 기다리면 도로는 비어 있습니다.

The mechanism is where vehicles wait. Whole-route reservation books the crane slot with the route; the vehicle waits at its last unloading point until its slot, the dock road stays free and the neighbouring sites stay reachable; the cost is longer waiting off-road and longer erection flow time. Segment request sends the vehicle to the dock edge to ask for the crane, and if it cannot get it the vehicle waits on the stopping road; in the current model road 161 is one resource, so the whole road is held. If the “where to wait” contrast survives physical rules, it becomes chapter 3's theoretical highlight, the dock-mouth proposition. Nominal crane load ρ is below 1, but vehicles waiting for the crane block the way to other stops, later erections cannot reach the dock, the crane idles because upstream is blocked, effective capacity drops below 1 and the system becomes unstable; with waiting off-road or the dock road used in sections, the stable range returns to ρ below 1. Its direct observable is the crane starvation rate added in T22. In theory this is blocking-induced starvation in queueing networks with blocking; the classic works in which every station has nominal load below 1 yet a policy destabilises the network serve as analogy and transfer sources, and fluid models are the proof tool. Those references are listed from memory and will be checked before citation. The small network has three resources, the crane, a single-lane dock road and one adjacent stop; T25 writes the threshold ρ* as a function of the dock road's slack, and we write the proposition ourselves.

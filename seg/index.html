这篇论文已发表在 Ocean Engineering。它回答的是：把船厂遥感影像分成道路、堆场、厂房和背景四类，换到一座训练时没见过的船厂，还能不能分得准、分得干净。我们在 SegFormer-B2 上加了两路只在训练时使用的约束，在五座留出船厂上 mIoU 从 0.6793 提高到 0.6907，而部署的模型和基线完全一样。

This paper is published in Ocean Engineering. It asks whether a model that splits shipyard imagery into roads, yards, buildings and background can still do so accurately and cleanly in a shipyard it never saw in training. We add two training-only constraints to SegFormer-B2; on five held-out yards mIoU rises from 0.6793 to 0.6907, and the deployed model is identical to the baseline.

이 논문은 Ocean Engineering에 게재되었습니다. 조선소 영상을 도로, 야드, 공장, 배경으로 나누는 모형이 학습 때 보지 못한 조선소에서도 정확하고 깔끔하게 나눌 수 있는지 묻습니다. SegFormer-B2에 학습 때만 쓰는 제약 두 가지를 더해, 미학습 조선소 5곳에서 mIoU가 0.6793에서 0.6907로 올랐고 배포 모형은 기준 모형과 같습니다.

1

问题The task과제

把船厂影像分成背景、道路、堆场开阔区、生产厂房四类。换一座船厂，布局和外观都变，类别交界变模糊、大区域内部变破碎。Split shipyard imagery into background, road, yard open area and production building. In another yard, layout and appearance change: class transitions blur and large regions fragment.조선소 영상을 배경, 도로, 야드 개방 구역, 생산 건물로 나눈다. 다른 조선소에서는 배치와 외관이 달라 경계가 흐려지고 큰 영역 내부가 조각난다.

2

做法The method방법

在 SegFormer-B2 上加两路训练约束：从标签形态学导出的边界约束，和 8 个可学习原型的区域一致性约束。推理时两路都去掉。Two training constraints on SegFormer-B2: a boundary constraint derived from the label morphology, and region consistency through eight learnable prototypes. Both are removed at inference.SegFormer-B2에 학습 제약 두 가지: 레이블 형태학에서 만든 경계 제약, 학습 가능한 원형 8개의 영역 일관성 제약. 추론 때는 둘 다 제거.

结果The results결과

All 10 site–assignment comparisons improve.미학습 조선소 5곳 × 시드 3개: mIoU 0.6793 → 0.6907, 경계 F1 0.7190 → 0.7393, 조각 비율 0.0775 → 0.0631. 10개 비교 모두 향상.

代价The cost비용

一分钟版本。任务是把船厂影像分成四类功能区；难点在换一座船厂之后，类别交界变模糊、大区域内部变破碎。做法是在 SegFormer-B2 上加两路只在训练时用的约束，边界约束管交界，区域一致性约束管内部。结果是五座留出船厂上三项重叠指标、边界指标和碎片率都改善，十组比较全部为正；推理时两路都去掉，部署模型零代价。

The one-minute version. The task splits shipyard imagery into four functional classes; the difficulty is that in a new yard class transitions blur and large regions fragment. We add two training-only constraints to SegFormer-B2: a boundary constraint for transitions and a region-consistency constraint for interiors. On five held-out yards, overlap, boundary and fragmentation all improve, with all ten comparisons positive; both branches are removed at inference, so deployment costs nothing.

The dataset covers eight yards in China and Korea: 247 source images of 1024 pixels cut into 988 patches at about 0.30 m, about 23 km² in all. Five yards have enough samples to serve as held-out test sites in turn; the other three contribute training images only. The four classes are roughly balanced. Three annotators labelled independently before review and adjudication, with pairwise mIoU of 0.921 on the QC subset.

The boundary constraint dilates and erodes each class mask once; the difference is that class's edge, and the union over classes is the target, so no extra labels are needed; a light head learns it under binary cross-entropy. Region consistency uses eight learnable prototypes: pixels are soft-assigned by cosine similarity, region features are aggregated and classified, then projected back as a region-level distribution, and KL pulls pixel predictions towards it. A fixed-random control isolates the effect of learning the prototypes.

这是博士论文的一个分项：占路运输模型和路网上界，对应论文第 2 章和第 3 章的上界节。问题很直接：载着分段的平板车把路占了，一座船厂的路网每天最多能完成多少运输任务？右边是我们的基本做法：一条路就是一个宽度容器，任何时刻占用的宽度之和不超过容量；把一天里的占用加起来，就得到线性规划的一行。这一版加进了最新的结果：没有抽样噪声的系统口径上界、对任何路由规则成立的路线自由上界，以及新放行规则与坞前道路分段下的逐例核对。

This is one sub-study of the thesis: the road-occupancy model and the network bound, which are chapter 2 and the bound section of chapter 3. The question is direct: once loaded transporters own the road, how many transport tasks can a yard's network complete in a day? On the right is our basic idea: a road is a width container, and at every moment the widths in use cannot exceed its capacity; summing the occupancy over a day gives one row of a linear program. This version adds the latest results: a system bound free of sampling noise, a route-free bound that holds for any routing rule, and the case-by-case check under the new release rule and the sectioned dock road.

模型The model모형

路段、路口、停靠位都是宽度容器，车按阻塞时间（通过 + 净空，路口加转弯扫过）占用；载货与空驶占宽不同。Segments, junctions and stops are width containers, held for a blocking time (passage plus clearance, plus the turn sweep at junctions); loaded and empty vehicles take different widths.구간, 교차로, 정차 위치는 폭 컨테이너이고, 차량은 차단 시간 (통과 + 여유, 교차로는 회전 시간 추가) 동안 점유한다. 적재와 공차의 점유 폭이 다르다.

路线自由上界Route-free bound경로 자유 상한

载货路线也由线性规划自选，对任何路由规则成立，带不依赖求解器容差的证书。上界中的路线余量从 0（入口取紧）到 +45%（贯通走廊）。Loaded routes are chosen by the LP too, so the bound holds for any routing rule, with a certificate that does not rest on solver tolerances. Route slack in the bound runs from 0 (an entrance binds) to +45% (a through corridor).적재 경로도 LP가 고르므로 어떤 경로 규칙에도 성립하며, 솔버 허용 오차에 기대지 않는 증서가 있다. 상한의 경로 여유는 0 (출입구 결속)에서 +45% (관통 통로)까지.

The one-minute version. The question: when loaded vehicles own the road, how many tasks a day can the network complete, with a bound that holds for a class of rules and shows where it binds. The model: segments, junctions and stops are width containers held for blocking times. The bound: we added three kinds of rows to the occupancy-rate LP, then computed expected holds directly from the sampling law of the task stream, giving a system bound with no sampling noise, 1,736 tasks a day on Okpo's main scenario and 1,298 on Yantai's; none of more than fifty thousand occupancy simulation points in two rounds of checks exceeds it. The route-free bound lets the LP choose the loaded routes itself, so it holds for any routing rule; how much freeing the routes adds runs from 0 to 45% depending on the kind of binding resource. Next, I re-check the lower edge of the interval with a less conservative deadlock-free rule.

There are four rules. Free flow is the field's default; it does not check capacity, so the bound does not apply to it. Whole-route reservation books the whole trip at once and cannot deadlock. Segment request books one piece at a time, and a deadlock sends one vehicle off the network for 900 seconds; this is the reference rule, used for comparison only. We built safe release: still segment by segment, but each grant first passes a banker's-algorithm safety check, so only requests that cannot lead to deadlock are granted and no teleport is needed. The network bound fixes the loaded routes to the simulation's shortest paths; the detour orchestration of chapter 4 sends vehicles around the binding road, and then the network bound no longer bounds it. The route-free bound makes loaded moves a multicommodity flow as well: a commodity is an origin, a destination and a width class, and the LP chooses the routes itself, with column generation pricing new routes on dual prices. Holds that do not depend on the route are counted exactly as in the network bound; the route-dependent items, vehicle length, turn sweep, the last stretch before the end stop and tail clearance, all take lower bounds, so whatever route a vehicle takes, the LP never counts too much.

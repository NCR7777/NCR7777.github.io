这是博士论文的一个分项：占路运输模型和路网上界，对应论文第 2 章和第 3 章的上界节。问题很直接：载着分段的平板车把路占了，一座船厂的路网每天最多能完成多少运输任务？右边是我们的基本做法：一条路就是一个宽度容器，任何时刻占用的宽度之和不超过容量；把一天里的占用加起来，就得到线性规划的一行。

This is one sub-study of the thesis: the road-occupancy model and the network bound, which are chapter 2 and the bound section of chapter 3. The question is direct: once loaded transporters own the road, how many transport tasks can a yard's network complete in a day? On the right is our basic idea: a road is a width container, and at every moment the widths in use cannot exceed its capacity; summing the occupancy over a day gives one row of a linear program.

Reservation, and segment request while no clearing occurs, are inside the proof; a teleport can cut a hold short, so the reference rule is only checked case by case; segment_safe has no teleport and satisfies the conditions by design, so after the T22 review it can come inside the proof.

This briefing covers chapter 4 of the thesis: traffic orchestration. The chapter 3 flag experiment shows that, where the network binds, capacity is an interval whose lower edge is set by the traffic rule. Chapter 4 designs deadlock-free task assignment and passage sequencing to push that edge up. Top right is the typical deadlock of naive segment request: two loaded vehicles enter a single lane head-on, each holding one segment and waiting for the other's. Bottom right is the idea of theorem T5: before a loaded departure, book the road up to the next safe holding point together with a slot there.

先看为什么要做编排。横轴是吞吐占路网上界 T1′ 的比例。整条路径预约是现在唯一既无死锁、又在定理范围内的规则，两座船厂的平台都只到 T1′ 的 36% 左右；逐段申请到 57% 和 50%，但它靠瞬移疏解，只能作参照，不能当下沿。从下沿到 100% 之间这一大段，就是第 4 章要挣回来的部分。同时第 3 章在加强上界，把上沿往下压，两头夹住真实运力。

Why orchestration. The axis is throughput as a share of the network bound T1′. Whole-route reservation is today's only rule that is both deadlock-free and inside the theorem; on both yards its plateau reaches only about 36% of T1′. Segment request reaches 57% and 50%, but it relies on teleport clearing, so it is a reference and cannot be the lower edge. The long stretch from the lower edge to 100% is what chapter 4 aims to win back, while chapter 3 tightens the bound from above, so the true capacity is squeezed from both sides.
